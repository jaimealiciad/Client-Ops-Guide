Communication & Project Management

📋
Internal Project Management

Kantata: Project plan referencing SOW | Owner: Strategic Account Director

Expectation: Time tracked weekly by EOD first business day of following week

GDrive/OneDrive: Internal folder for all documents, drafts, notes, deliverables

Expectation: Organized weekly | Nothing on personal drives

🗓️
Weekly Internal Sync

INTERNAL 30-60 min | 24-48 hrs before client sync

Attendees: Core internal project team

Outcomes:

  • Confirm client facing call agenda (GTM updates invite)
  • Confirm roles & responsibilities for call
  • Review metrics and discussion points
  • Update Kantata project
Internal RoB Template
💬
Weekly Client Sync

CLIENT FACING 30-60 min

Attendees: SAD (leads & facilitates), GTM (supports & records), Sales (as needed), Client team

Outcomes:

  • SAD reviews agenda and leads discussion
  • Call recap sent within 3 hours (or next morning for EOD meetings)
  • Recap saved in shared locations
